# What Remains

_A dark, first-person survival action RPG about death, stagnation, and the right to an ending._

## About

**What Remains** is a survival action RPG set in a world where resources are finite, magic is fading, and humanity has been reduced to a tool of those who control what remains.

The game is being developed primarily for **Terra**, as a connected project within its setting and lore.

During the Great War, the Overlords enslaved humanity through their control over the world's dwindling resources and the magic tied to them. Their rule has endured, but everything has an end. The player takes the role of the one who will bring that ending.

The central mechanic is resource scarcity. Resources are finite, and their depletion has consequences beyond ordinary survival. Magic depends on what remains in the world, making every resource a part of a much larger struggle.

## Gameplay

The current version focuses on survival, exploration, crafting, and combat.

- **Survival:** Maintain your body's needs, including hydration and satiation. Neglecting them brings temporary penalties.
- **Mind and spirit:** Rest and find moments of peace to maintain your mental and spiritual condition.
- **Exploration:** Travel through a procedurally generated wasteland and search for useful resources.
- **Crafting:** Discover recipes and create weapons, tools, and structures.
- **Combat:** Fight hostile creatures using crafted weapons.
- **Building:** Establish campfires, Bone Totems, and other useful structures.
- **Finite resources:** What you consume or collect matters. The world is not an endless supply of materials.

## Development

The project is in active development.

The current version uses an ASCII-based presentation and runs in a web environment. There is currently no active public website.

Planned future improvements include:

- An online web version.
- Expanded survival, crafting, exploration, and combat systems.
- More world structures, enemies, and resources.
- A graphical upgrade, potentially using pixel art.

These plans are not promises of release dates or guaranteed features.

## Usage and licensing
